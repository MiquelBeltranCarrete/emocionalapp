# GOOGLE_PLAY_SETUP.md

## Step-by-Step Instructions for Setting Up the 'Educacion Emocional Infancia y Adolescencia' App in Google Play Console

### 1. Create a Google Play Developer Account
- Go to the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
- Sign in with your Google account or create a new one if necessary.
- Follow the steps to set up your developer account, including paying the one-time registration fee.

### 2. Create a New Application
- In the Google Play Console, navigate to the "All apps" section.
- Click on the "Create app" button.
- Choose the application type (e.g., App, Game).
- Fill in the app details including the name (Educacion Emocional Infancia y Adolescencia) and select the default language.
- Click "Create" to proceed.

### 3. Fill Out the Store Listing
- Navigate to the "Store presence" -> "Main store listing".
- Complete the following fields:
  - **App Title**: Educacion Emocional Infancia y Adolescencia
  - **Short Description**: A brief overview of your app.
  - **Full Description**: Detailed description of the app’s features and benefits.
  - **Screenshots**: Upload images that showcase the app’s functionality.
  - **Feature Graphic**: Required for promotions in the Play Store.
  - **Application Icon**: The icon that will represent your app.
  - **Categorization**: Select the appropriate category for your app.
- Provide the necessary information legally required in the content rating section.

### 4. Upload Assets
- Go to "App Integrity" and upload your app's APK or App Bundle.
- Ensure that you follow the guidelines for asset sizes and formats.
- Upload additional assets such as graphics and promotional images in the specified formats.

### 5. Set Up Pricing and Distribution
- Navigate to "Pricing & distribution".
- Set your app's pricing: Free or Paid.
- Select the countries where your app will be available.
- Decide if you want to enable certain features like in-app purchases or ads.

### 6. Submit for Review
- Ensure all the mandatory fields are filled out correctly.
- Click on "Review and rollout to production".
- Follow prompts to review your app submission.
- If everything is correct, click "Start rollout" to submit your app for review.

### 7. Monitor Your App’s Status
- After submission, monitor your app's status in the Google Play Console.  
- Be prepared to make any necessary changes if your app is rejected, and resubmit as needed.

## Conclusion
Following these steps will help you successfully set up and submit your app, Educacion Emocional Infancia y Adolescencia, on the Google Play Console.
